# Wiki Configuration

## Scope

This wiki documents the Lambda Engine cognitive architecture: its physics-flavored
core equation, phase-space states (J=0 / S* / ∅), the 20 Controlled Rupture
Operators, the dissipation calculus used to gauge reasoning risk, and the HALIRA
7-step protocol for paradigm-level contradictions — presented as a general
reasoning pattern any AI agent can adopt, not tied to any specific IDE, tool
surface, or slash-command set.

## Conventions

Primary source material originates from the `lazy-dev` repository's
`.cursor/rules/` and `.cursor/skills/` implementation, where this architecture
is expressed as Cursor-bound rules with concrete tool bindings (grep, read_file,
codebase_search, etc.). Compiled articles here strip the tool-specific bindings
and generalize the underlying reasoning discipline so it applies to any LLM-based
agent, framework, or multi-agent protocol.
